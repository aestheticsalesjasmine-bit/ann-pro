Straight answers

The questions a careful buyer asks.

Is this legal advice?

No. ANN-PRO provides regulatory monitoring, documentation and verification. It does not provide legal representation or opinions, and it does not guarantee any board or agency outcome. We work alongside your attorney and hand off a documented record.

What exactly is a "competency," and how is it different from my course certificate?

A certificate says you attended. A competency is a validated, observed demonstration of a specific skill in a specific setting — assessed by someone qualified, documented with who validated it, how, and when, and re-validated on a schedule. It's the standard accreditation bodies actually look for, and it's the piece our Vault is built around.

What does the verification record actually certify?

That specific documents, licenses and CE hours were reviewed on a stated date against a stated rule. It's a dated record of what was checked — not a warranty of compliance, and not an accreditation.

How fast can we produce records if a board or payer asks?

The benchmark we build to: any three staff files — licenses, CE, competencies, attestations — complete and current, exported in under five minutes. That's the difference between a folder and a system.

Who can see our documents?

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and credential documents without patient data are not medical records. We never sell, share, or train on your documents. Ever.

How is this different from a template or a law firm?

A template gives you a cover. A firm gives you counsel by the hour. This gives you documents written to your specific state and menu by a nurse who runs a practice under the same rules, then keeps them current as those rules move.

Do you house our collaborative agreements?

Only if you want them housed. They're legal instruments, so they live in a separate practice-legal drawer — stored as you provide them, never written, reviewed or endorsed by us, exported exactly as received.

Does the CE count toward license renewal?

Accredited CE is delivered under board-approved provider #18009. Practice rounds are reinforcement, not accredited hours, and are labelled that way.

Do your courses include the required Implicit Bias content?

Yes — every CA BRN course includes Implicit Bias content, required since 2023. It's built into the curriculum, not a separate add-on. Certificates generate instantly on completion, available 24/7.

Are the courses accepted outside California?

Our CA CEP courses are accepted by many state boards of nursing nationwide — check with your board for its specific acceptance rules, and the acceptance table on the CE site before purchasing.
